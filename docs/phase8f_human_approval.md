# Phase 8F: Human Approval Gate

## 1. Overview & Objective

Phase 8F acts as the strict authorization gate between AI inspection task recommendations and operational execution. No recommendation generated by the system can become an active operational task or enter the inspection queue without explicit, authenticated human engineering approval.

## 2. Approval Lifecycle States

- `PENDING`: Initial state when an AI recommendation is submitted to the engineering approval queue.
- `APPROVED`: Human engineer approves the recommendation in its original form. Instantiates an active `InspectionTaskModel` in `CREATED` state.
- `MODIFIED`: Human engineer adjusts priority, recommended timing window, or task type before authorization. The modifications and original recommendation are both preserved in the audit record.
- `REJECTED`: Human engineer declines the recommendation. No task is instantiated. The rejection rationale and reviewer details are permanently retained in the audit ledger.

## 3. Auditing & Safety Guarantees

- **Full Provenance:** Every approval record links the original recommendation payload, reviewer identifier, timestamp, and optional modification diff.
- **Single-Disposition Invariance:** Once an approval is marked `APPROVED`, `MODIFIED`, or `REJECTED`, attempting to re-process it raises `ApprovalAlreadyProcessedError`.
- **Zero Automated Dispatch:** Recommendations cannot bypass the gatekeeper.
